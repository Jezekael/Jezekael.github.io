APT31 is one of the clearest examples of a China-linked group described in both private sector and
government language as a long-running cyber espionage actor. U.S. Department of Justice material
states that the APT31 Group was part of a cyberespionage program run by the MSS Hubei State Security
Department and had targeted thousands of individuals and companies since at least 2010.
