UNC4841 came into public view through the exploitation of Barracuda Email Security Gateway
appliances. Mandiant assessed with high confidence that the actor is engaged in espionage in support
of the People's Republic of China. The campaign is notable for using network appliances as a covert
access layer, then adapting malware and persistence quickly in response to remediation.
