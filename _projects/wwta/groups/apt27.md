APT27 is a long-established China-linked intrusion set that has appeared in public reporting for
years under multiple vendor names. In 2025, U.S. Department of Justice charging material tied
alleged Chinese hackers with PRC government links to a years-long campaign targeting U.S. technology
firms, municipalities, universities, defense contractors, think tanks, and other organizations for
theft and later resale of data.
