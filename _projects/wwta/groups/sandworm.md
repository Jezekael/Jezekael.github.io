Sandworm is one of the most consistently publicly attributed Russian military cyber actors. Joint
advisories from the UK and U.S. governments connect Sandworm to GRU activity and link the actor to
operations ranging from NotPetya to Cyclops Blink and other infrastructure-focused campaigns. Public
reporting often shows a blend of espionage access, sabotage and strategic signaling.
