Lazarus is one of the best known DPRK cyber group names in public reporting. The U.S. Treasury
formally identified Lazarus, Bluenoroff and Andariel as agencies, instrumentalities or controlled
entities of the Government of North Korea based on their relationship to the Reconnaissance General
Bureau. Public casework spans WannaCry, destructive intrusions, financial theft and supply-chain
activity.
