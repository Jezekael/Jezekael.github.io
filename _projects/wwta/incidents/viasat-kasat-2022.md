The KA-SAT incident took place around one hour before Russia’s full-scale invasion of Ukraine.
Public government statements describe broad communication outages affecting public authorities,
businesses and private users, not just targets in Ukraine. The event is now treated as one of the
clearest examples of strategic cyber disruption synchronized with kinetic military operations.
