Public U.S. government statements described a broad campaign in which PRC-affiliated actors accessed
commercial telecommunications infrastructure. The operation stands out because telecom environments
provide both sensitive content and privileged network vantage points. Later advisory language
connected the activity to wider reporting commonly referred to as Salt Typhoon, framing the campaign
as part of a sustained espionage effort rather than a one-off intrusion.
