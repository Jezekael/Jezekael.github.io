APT41 is unusual in public reporting because it has been associated with both state-linked espionage
and financially motivated activity. FBI material identifies named defendants as members of
APT41/BARIUM and describes intrusions into hundreds of companies across sectors such as
telecommunications, government, defense, social media, education, and manufacturing, including
supply-chain attacks and ransomware deployment.
