The 2025 DOJ unsealing around APT27-linked activity is notable because it frames the operation as
part of a wider state-protected hacker-for-hire ecosystem. According to the allegations, the
defendants stole data from numerous U.S. entities, caused millions of dollars in damages, and later
brokered stolen information for sale. The public record also emphasizes how Chinese state organs
allegedly directed or financed intrusions while allowing the same operators to monetize access and
stolen data.
