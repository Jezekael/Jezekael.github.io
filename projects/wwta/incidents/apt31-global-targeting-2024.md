The public allegations against APT31 describe a broad campaign that blends political surveillance,
transnational repression support, and traditional economic espionage. DOJ says the group sent more
than 10,000 malicious emails, used hidden tracking links to gather targeting data, and then pursued
more direct compromises of routers, devices, mailboxes, and enterprise networks. The case material
gives unusual detail on the scale and diversity of APT31 targeting.
