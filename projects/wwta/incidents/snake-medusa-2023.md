Operation MEDUSA did not mark the start of the Snake campaign, but it is a reliable public anchor
for documenting it. According to the DOJ, Turla used versions of Snake for nearly 20 years to steal
sensitive documents from victims in at least 50 countries and to relay exfiltration traffic through
a covert peer-to-peer network of compromised machines.
