APT40 is a China-linked espionage actor that has repeatedly targeted governments and organizations
in the Asia-Pacific region. A 2024 joint advisory from CISA, the FBI, NSA, ACSC, and partners
described the group's tradecraft in action and noted its habit of rapidly weaponizing public exploit
proof-of-concepts against vulnerable public-facing infrastructure.
