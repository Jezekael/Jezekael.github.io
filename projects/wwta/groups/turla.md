Turla is publicly tied by U.S. authorities to a unit within Center 16 of Russia’s Federal Security
Service. The group is widely known for long-lived espionage campaigns, covert routing infrastructure
and carefully engineered malware families used against governments, diplomatic entities, journalists
and research targets.
