Salt Typhoon is the industry name most commonly used for a PRC-linked espionage cluster associated
with sustained access to telecommunications infrastructure. U.S. government reporting in late 2024
and 2025 described unauthorized access to commercial telecommunications providers by PRC-affiliated
actors, and later advisory language noted partial overlap with activity commonly referred to as Salt
Typhoon. The public picture emphasizes strategic intelligence collection, infrastructure access, and
long-term operational persistence rather than noisy disruption.
