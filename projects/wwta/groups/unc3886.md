Mandiant has repeatedly described UNC3886 as a highly advanced China-nexus espionage group with
strong tradecraft around virtualization and appliance compromises. The group stands out for
targeting vCenter, ESXi and other difficult-to-monitor layers, then using custom backdoors and
careful post-compromise steps to protect access.
