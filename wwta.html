Attribution & sources

Actor profiles and incident attribution here are summarized from public U.S./UK/EU government advisories (CISA, FBI, NSA, NCSC, DOJ), EU Council declarations, and named vendor investigations (Microsoft, Mandiant). For a richer dataset, this page is designed to be extended with MITRE ATT&CK Groups, the CFR Cyber Operations Tracker, ETDA Threat Group Cards and the MISP galaxy , see CHECKLIST.md and STACK_DECISION.md in the repo.
